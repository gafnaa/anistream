# Code of Conduct

This is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers, pledge to make taking part in this project
a harassment-free experience for everyone. This holds regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, including in code review
- Accepting responsibility for our mistakes, apologizing to those affected, and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people working on it

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Sharing this repository's code, credentials, or infrastructure details outside the project without permission (see [LICENSE](LICENSE))
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, code
review, commit messages, and any channel used to discuss the project. It also
applies when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

These standards apply to automated contributors as well. AI agents working in
this repo follow [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md), and the person running the agent is
responsible for what it submits.

## Enforcement

To report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior, contact the
project maintainer, [@gafnaa](https://github.com/gafnaa), directly on GitHub.
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The
maintainer will respect the privacy and security of the person who reports an
incident.

The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct. The maintainer may
also remove anyone's access to the repository, temporarily or permanently, for
behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

### Enforcement guidelines

1. **Correction.** For inappropriate language or other unprofessional behavior,
   the maintainer sends a private written warning that explains why the
   behavior was inappropriate.
2. **Warning.** For a single serious incident or repeated issues, the
   maintainer issues a warning with consequences if the behavior continues.
3. **Temporary ban.** For a serious violation or sustained inappropriate
   behavior, the maintainer temporarily removes access to the repository.
4. **Permanent ban.** For a pattern of violations, harassment, or aggression
   toward others, the maintainer permanently removes access.
